## 2. OVERLOOKED PILLAR 2: LEGAL & PDP ACT (UU PDP) COMPLIANCE

* **Indonesia PDP Act (UU No. 27/2022) Compliance**:
  - Explicit user consent for data collection.
  - Zero sale or monetization of user financial transaction data.
  - Right to erasure ("Delete My Workspace & All Data" 1-click button).
* **Non-Advisory Regulatory Disclaimer**:
  - *"SAKU is an analytics and data management tool. SAKU does not provide investment, tax, or legal advice."*

---

## 3. OVERLOOKED PILLAR 3: DATA BACKUP, PORTABILITY & ZERO LOCK-IN

* **Encrypted Portable Backup (`.saku` Bundle)**:
  - Users can generate an AES-256 encrypted `.saku` backup bundle at any time.
  - Seamless restoration when switching devices or reinstalling the app.
* **1-Click Open Export**:
  - Complete data export to CSV and JSON formats with zero vendor lock-in.

## 4. OVERLOOKED PILLAR 4: COMMUNITY PLUGIN SYSTEM & EXTENSIBILITY

* **Community Statement Parser Plugins**:

* **Webhook & Automation API**:
