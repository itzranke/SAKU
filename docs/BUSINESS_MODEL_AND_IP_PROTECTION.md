# 💰 SAKU BUSINESS MODEL, COMMERCIAL IP PROTECTION, & AUTH STRATEGY

---

## 1. REFINED MONETIZATION: PURE MONTHLY SAAS & FREEMIUM HOOK

SAKU adopts a **Pure Monthly Recurring Subscription Model (SaaS)**. Lifetime licenses are explicitly excluded to guarantee perpetual recurring revenue (MRR/ARR) and prevent long-term revenue decay.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ FREEMIUM HOOK CYCLE (Nir Eyal "Hooked" Framework)                          │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. TRIGGER       : Daily micro-expense notification or evening check-in.   │
│ 2. ACTION        : 1-Tap Fast Ingestion or WhatsApp/Telegram Bot message.  │
│ 3. REWARD        : Instant Net Worth clarity & zero math error assurance.  │
│ 4. INVESTMENT    : User's financial history accumulates, raising switching  │
│                    costs to 100%.                                           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Free Tier Hook**: Includes core Net Worth tracking, basic cash/bank accounts, and 1-tap logging. Essential enough to become an indispensable daily habit.
* **Pro Tier (Monthly Subscription)**: Unlocks multi-wallet sync, live MT5 local bridge, automated bot capture, and advanced health analytics.

## 3. FRICTIONLESS AUTHENTICATION (EMAIL & WHATSAPP OTP)

To maximize onboarding conversion in SEA/Indonesia:
* **Passwordless OTP Authentication**:
  - Verification codes sent directly to **User Email** or **WhatsApp Chat**.
  - Eliminates forgotten password friction and raises onboarding conversion by up to 40%.

## 4. COMMERCIAL IP PROTECTION & B2B COLLABORATION STRATEGY

To prevent competitors from stealing SAKU's code for free while protecting enterprise partnership channels:
* **Closed Proprietary Core**: Core double-entry ledger algorithms, MT5 bridge protocols, and statement parsers remain **100% Proprietary Closed-Source IP**.
* **B2B Partnership Channel**: Commercial API access for financial institutions and business partners is delivered under formal B2B licensing agreements.
