## 1. CORE ACCOUNTING CONCEPTS & RULES

### 1.1 Fundamental Accounting Equation
$$\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity}$$

* **Assets (Aset)**: Resource with economic value owned by the user (Cash, Bank, Investments, Receivables, Physical Assets).
* **Liabilities (Liabilitas)**: Obligations owed to others (Credit Cards, PayLater, KPR, Personal Loans, Informal Debts).
* **Equity / Net Worth (Ekuitas / Kekayaan Bersih)**: The residual interest in assets after deducting liabilities.

### 1.2 Double-Entry Bookkeeping Principles
Every financial event requires a debit and credit entry of equal magnitude:
* **Asset Increase**: DEBIT
* **Asset Decrease**: CREDIT
* **Liability Increase**: CREDIT
* **Liability Decrease**: DEBIT
* **Income Increase**: CREDIT
* **Expense Increase**: DEBIT

## 2. INVESTMENT & PORTFOLIO METRICS GLOSSARY

* **FIFO (First-In, First-Out)**: Cost basis method where the earliest purchased assets are matched against sells to calculate capital gains.
* **Weighted Average Cost (WAC)**: Cost basis calculated as Total Capital Invested divided by Total Units Held.
* **Realized P&L**: Capital gain or loss locked in upon selling an asset.
* **Unrealized P&L (Floating P&L)**: Paper gain or loss based on current market price vs. cost basis.
* **Dividend Yield**: Annual dividend payouts divided by current market asset value.

## 3. ACTIVE TRADING & METATRADER GLOSSARY

* **Balance**: The realized cash balance of a trading account (excludes open positions).
* **Equity**: Floating real-time value = $\text{Balance} + \text{Unrealized P\&L}$.
* **Margin**: Collateral required to keep leveraged open positions active.
* **Free Margin**: $\text{Equity} - \text{Margin}$ available for opening new trades.
* **Margin Level %**: $\left( \frac{\text{Equity}}{\text{Margin}} \right) \times 100\%$.
* **Profit Factor**: Total Gross Profits divided by Total Gross Losses.
* **Expectancy**: Average amount won or lost per trade = $(\text{Win Rate} \times \text{Avg Win}) - (\text{Loss Rate} \times \text{Avg Loss})$.
* **MFE (Maximum Favorable Excursion)**: The maximum peak profit reached by a trade before closing.
* **MAE (Maximum Adverse Excursion)**: The maximum peak drawdown experienced by a trade before closing.
