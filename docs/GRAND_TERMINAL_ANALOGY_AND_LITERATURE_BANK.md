## 2. LITERATURE BANK & FOUNDATIONAL CLASSICS

SAKU incorporates core principles from the world's most influential financial & trading literature:

### 2.1 Personal Finance & Net Worth Classics
1. **"Your Money or Your Life" (Vicki Robin & Joe Dominguez)**:
   - *Core Concept*: Net Worth as the ultimate metric of Financial Independence (FIRE).
   - *SAKU Integration*: Real-time Net Worth Evolution chart and Liquid Cash ratio.
2. **"The Total Money Makeover" (Dave Ramsey)**:
   - *Core Concept*: Debt Snowball method for paying off informal debts and loans.
   - *SAKU Integration*: Debt & Installment Tracker with 1-tap payoff simulator.
3. **"I Will Teach You To Be Rich" (Ramit Sethi)**:
   - *Core Concept*: Automated money flow & conscious spending (50/30/20 Rule).
   - *SAKU Integration*: Automated Envelope & Category Budgeting rules.

### 2.2 Investment & Portfolio Management Classics
1. **"The Intelligent Investor" (Benjamin Graham)**:
   - *Core Concept*: Margin of Safety and Cost Basis tracking.
   - *SAKU Integration*: FIFO Cost Basis and Floating vs Realized P&L.
2. **"Common Sense on Mutual Funds" (John Bogle)**:
   - *Core Concept*: Asset Allocation & Compound Dividend Reinvestment.
   - *SAKU Integration*: Multi-Asset Class Holdings breakdown (Stocks, Mutual Funds, Bonds, Crypto).

### 2.3 Quantitative Active Trading & Psychology Classics
1. **"Trade Your Way to Financial Freedom" (Dr. Van Tharp)**:
   - *Core Concept*: System Expectancy, R-Multiple distribution, and Position Sizing.
   - *SAKU Integration*: MT5 Trade Journal metrics (Expectancy, Profit Factor, Win Rate %).
2. **"Trading in the Zone" (Mark Douglas)**:
   - *Core Concept*: Psychological state correlation and discipline tracking.
   - *SAKU Integration*: Trade Tagging (`#FOMO`, `#BREAKOUT`, `#PLAN_EXECUTED`).

### 2.4 Accounting Standards Literature
1. **IAS 21 (International Accounting Standard - FX Rates)**:
   - *Core Concept*: Functional currency translation and unrealized FX gain/loss revaluation.
   - *SAKU Integration*: Daily FX Revaluation Engine and FX Spread Expense logging.
